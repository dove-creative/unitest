## Scope

This table collects the existing checks in LabTest, LabExtensionTest, and ToUncontinuableTest. It covers standalone Lab execution, extension assembly, and integration contracts at termination.
The 18 contract groups correspond to 141 + 10 + 22 = 173 NUnit cases. Termination checks involving CompositeLab and Project are counted only here.

## Scope separation

UL02 and UL05 apply the presence or absence of constructor configuration to 31 stage-condition combinations, producing 62 cases each.
The UL04 inputs are (-1,-1), (-1,3), (3,-1), (1,10), (10,1), and (0,5).
UL09 declares termination in Template, Arrange, Act, or Assert. UL11 combines three layers with declaration in the template or Assert.
Actual repeated paths and independent scenario-list checks are covered separately by the [UniTest Lab table](../../UniTest/Lab/01.%20Tables.md).
