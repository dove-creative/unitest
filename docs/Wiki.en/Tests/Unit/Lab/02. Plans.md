## Verification criteria

Execution-failure cases check the stage and inner cause of ExecutionException. Extension cases compare the actual invocation sequence of each callback and the counters for Actors that are not executed.
Termination cases check that the model can still continue during the current Assert, then check termination after completion.
UL14 combines ThreadPool/Inline with Lab/Compact/Composite and checks design-call count, ProcessCount, child count, and terminal Success.
Statistics are aggregated once per fixture and are not counted again in the [CompositeLab unit documentation](../CompositeLab/02.%20Plans.md).
Existing evidence is in the [result record](03.%20Results.md).
