## Contents

## Scope

This table maps the existing ModelTest contracts for state transitions, execution history, and remaining execution counts.
The 14 contract groups in the table and the 36 NUnit cases after parameter expansion are separate counts.

## States and actions

| ID | State/input | Action | Current verification | Cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UM01 | Initial state | Switch to unsustainable, noncontinuable, or count-limited state | Check combinations of Sustainable, Continuable, and RemainingExecutionCount | 3 |
| UM02 | Unlimited and continuable | Execute once or twice | Preserve sustainability and create execution history in input ID order | 4 |
| UM03 | Count-limited and continuable | Execute with 10 or 1 remaining executions | Continuable at 9, noncontinuable at 0, and history recorded | 2 |
| UM04 | Noncontinuable | Attempt execution | InvalidOperationException | 1 |
| UM05 | Initial, unsustainable, count-limited, or noncontinuable | Set Sustainable to false | Set or retain unsustainability and preserve other restrictions | 4 |
| UM06 | Initial, unsustainable, count-limited, or noncontinuable | Set Continuable to false | Set or retain unsustainability and noncontinuability | 4 |
| UM07 | The four states above | Set Continuable to true | InvalidOperationException | 4 |
| UM08 | The four states above | Set RemainingExecutionCount to null | InvalidOperationException | 4 |
| UM09 | Unlimited and continuable | Set remaining count to 10 or 0 | Unsustainable, and noncontinuable at 0 | 4 |
| UM10 | 10 remaining executions and continuable | Attempt to increase to 11 | Retain the limit of 10 | 1 |
| UM11 | Unlimited and noncontinuable | Set a limit of 10 | Apply the limit and preserve the terminated state | 1 |
| UM12 | 10 remaining executions and continuable | Reduce to 5 or 0 | Apply the smaller limit and terminate at 0 | 2 |
| M02 | 10 remaining executions and noncontinuable | Reduce to 5 | Retain the limit of 5 and the terminated state | 1 |
| M03 | 10 remaining executions and noncontinuable | Reduce to 0 | Retain the limit of 0 and the terminated state | 1 |

## Separation from self-tests

M02 and M03 are NUnit-only cases that retain their earlier enhancement IDs. They were separated from the [UniTest Model documentation](../../UniTest/Model/01.%20Tables.md) into this document.
M01 belongs to Recursion's ZeroRemainingCountReplay and remains under UniTest. Repeated paths and missing-candidate checks belong to those self-tests.
This table describes the current unit test assertions; it does not imply coverage of every input boundary.
