## Target and fixture

The target is [CompositeLabTest](../../../../../tests/BlackThunder.UniTest.Tests.Unit/CompositeLabTest.cs). SetUp prepares three Labs, a CompositeLab of the first two Labs, and a new Model.
SetLabs injects stage-specific failures into the two selected Labs, and TearDown clears the references.
This plan documents the methods and input generators already implemented. Additional verification is recorded separately in the [shared results](../../Result.md).

## Verification boundaries

UC05 checks behavior and error outcomes after extension. It does not separately assert persistent preservation of existing composites or every call order.
Composites with expected exceptions declared on multiple layers are covered by [UniTest CompositeLab](../../UniTest/CompositeLab/02.%20Plans.md).
Those self-test results are not added to the unit test pass count. Existing results are kept in the [execution record](03.%20Results.md).
