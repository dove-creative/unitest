## Scope

This document organizes the Lab generation, child-node preparation, cancellation, and single-use contracts checked by the existing TestDesignerTest.
It maps the coverage of existing NUnit unit tests rather than directing the creation of new tests.
Component responsibilities follow the Project execution and shutdown section of [Implementation](../../../02.%20Implementations.md).

Parent conditions are Root or an executed Success node. Child replay-failure conditions use only a Success parent.
'Preserve the parent' below covers status, depth, parent/child links, exception, Lab reference, ID, and model presence.
