## Verification limitations

TE04 checks the exception type delivered to the caller; it does not check every error field on the node.
TE13 and TE14 check rejection of duplicate calls without waiting for the first call to complete;
they do not guarantee that the first task always remains at a particular execution point.
TE21–TE24 only check the absence of exceptions when cancellation is requested after completion.
ProcessCount, full-project shutdown waiting, and worker cleanup are not directly asserted in this file.
