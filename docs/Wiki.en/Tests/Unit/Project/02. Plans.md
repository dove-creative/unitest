## Verification method

Results are checked through public node status, exceptions, execution history, and ProcessCount.
Work-lifetime checks also inspect the internal task list to ensure no unfinished work remains after return.
The work remains blocked at a gate throughout the 100ms shutdown-wait observation interval; this is not a delay used to guess cancellation timing.
The finally block releases the gate, waits for execution and worker termination, and restores the replaced global Logger.

The four fixtures above contain 24 + 9 + 9 + 21 = 63 cases.
The verified outcomes from the earlier run are in the [result record](03.%20Results.md).
